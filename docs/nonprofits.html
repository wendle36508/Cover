The problem

Most nonprofits can't cover what it costs to keep going.

36%

of US nonprofits ran a deficit in 2024, the most in 10 years of the survey.

52%

had three months of cash or less on hand.

81%

say raising money that covers their full costs is a challenge.

Source: Nonprofit Finance Fund, 2025 State of the Nonprofit Sector Survey (2,206 nonprofits).

What you get

Keep sponsors close without extra work.

Updates in one click.

Post a photo and a line about a dog or a program. Cover emails it to everyone following, and tells them when something wraps up so they can pick someone new.

  • No newsletters to write
  • An adoption or a full shelf becomes good news to share
  • A reminder from Cover before each update is due

Local businesses too.

Businesses can sponsor and get thanked where their customers can see it. You see every sponsor, person or business, in one simple list.

  • Business names shown on your page
  • A thank-you wall for sponsors who say yes
  • Know what's coming in each month
