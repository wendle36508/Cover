Privacy policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Cover is software that lets nonprofits take monthly sponsorships. It's run by Tanner Wendle. This page explains what information Cover collects, why, and who sees it. Questions: hello@covergiving.com.

If you sponsor a nonprofit

We collect:

  • Your first name and email address.
  • Your business name, if you sponsor as a business.
  • What you chose to follow, your monthly amount, and whether you said your name can appear on the page.

Your card details never touch Cover. You type them into a secure form from Stripe, our payment processor, and Stripe handles them under Stripe's privacy policy.

If you run a nonprofit on Cover

We collect your email address (to sign you in) and your nonprofit's details and photos (to build your page). To pay you, Stripe collects your organization's tax ID, bank account and a representative's identity, as the law requires for anyone receiving payments. Cover doesn't store your bank or identity details.

How we use it

  • To run your monthly sponsorship and show it on the nonprofit's page.
  • To show your first name or business name on the page, only if you said yes. Otherwise you appear as "A neighbor."
  • To let the nonprofit you sponsor see your name and email, so they can thank you and send you updates.
  • To send sign-in links and messages about your account or sponsorship.

Who sees it

  • The nonprofit you sponsor. Only its own team, never other nonprofits.
  • Stripe, which processes payments.
  • Supabase, which hosts Cover's database, and our email provider, which sends sign-in links. They only use it to provide those services to us.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use advertising trackers.

What's stored in your browser

If you sign in, your browser keeps your sign-in session. Cover doesn't use advertising cookies.

Keeping and deleting it

We keep your information while your sponsorship or account is active. Email us to see or delete what Cover has about you. Nonprofits and Stripe may need to keep payment records for tax and legal reasons.

Children

Cover isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. For big changes, we'll tell nonprofits and active sponsors by email.
