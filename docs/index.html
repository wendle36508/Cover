Updates that write themselves

Keep sponsors close without adding work.

Post a photo or a one-line note. Cover turns it into a personal monthly update for everyone following, signed by the program or animal they picked.

  • One click from your team, no newsletters to write
  • Milestones become celebrations: an adoption, a graduation, a full shelf
  • When something wraps up, sponsors get a new one to follow, so the gift keeps going
