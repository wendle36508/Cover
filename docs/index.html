How it works

Three steps, about a minute.

01

Pick one.

Choose a dog, a program or a room to follow.

02

Give monthly.

Any amount from $5, monthly or one time. For you, or as a gift.

03

Get updates.

A photo certificate right away, then photos and news from the nonprofit.

What you get

More than a thank-you.

  • A photo certificate to keep or print
  • Photo updates and news about the one you follow
  • A note if your dog is adopted or your program wraps up, and the chance to pick someone new
  • Change or cancel anytime on My sponsorships

Where your money goes

Straight to the nonprofit. Cover never holds donated money. Every gift goes into one fund for everyone the nonprofit cares for, and the one you pick is who you hear about.
