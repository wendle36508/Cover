Cover logo directions: grassroots

Goal: feel like neighbors helping a cause, not a bank. Warmer colors, softer shapes, a little handmade. Each card shows the full logo and the small app-icon version. Pick a few favorites and I'll refine those.

Terracotta #D9653BMustard #E3A93BForest #2F5D46Sky #6FA3C7Rose #E58A8ASage #9DB59ACream #FBF6ECInk #2B2622
Today's logo, for comparison: heavy wordmark, crisp check. Reads more "fintech."
01Patchwork quilt

A quilt is what a community makes to cover someone. Each patch is a neighbor; together they keep a cause warm.

02Heart in the "o"

Chunky, friendly letters with a heart where the "o" goes. Instantly says giving, not banking.

03A roof over it

"Cover" as shelter: a hand-drawn roof over the word. Works for rescues, pantries, shelters, youth clubs alike.

04Community stamp

Feels like a farmers-market stamp or a co-op seal. Local, trustworthy, a bit old-school.

05Cupped hands

Two hands (donor and nonprofit) holding a heart together. The classic giving image, kept simple.

06Sprout from the "v"

Small monthly gifts that grow. A soft serif with a seedling sprouting from the "v".

07Handwritten note

Like the thank-you card a rescue writes by hand. Personal, warm, impossible to mistake for a bank.

08Marker poster

Hand-lettered rally-poster energy: a community drive, a bake sale banner, people showing up.

09Crowd heart

Many small dots, many small donors, make one heart. Says "this is a crowd of neighbors."

10Folded blanket

A warm blanket with hand stitching: comfort, shelter, care. Quietly playful, very "donor."

11Neighborhood

A little street of houses, one with a heart in the window. Local causes, local people.

12Speech-bubble heart

Updates are the point: you hear from the dog or program you follow. A heart in a message bubble.

13Two circles meet

A giver and a cause overlap; where they meet, something warmer appears. Simple and grown-up.

14Shelter tent

A small shelter with the door open. Outdoorsy, scrappy, the opposite of a glass office tower.

15Heart becomes the "v"

Keeps today's idea (a symbol replaces the "v") but trades the cold check mark for a warm heart. Easiest switch.

16Laptop sticker

Looks like a sticker volunteers slap on water bottles. Playful, young, made to be shared.
